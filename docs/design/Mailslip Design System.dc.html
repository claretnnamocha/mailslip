Design System · v1.0

One calm system,
light and dark.

The tokens, type, icons and components behind Mailslip. Every surface is shown in both themes: the accent holds steady, the neutrals invert, and email content stays on a white sheet even in the dark.

03

Iconography

One line-icon family: 24px grid, 1.7 stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in currentColor so a single glyph works in both themes and inherits state.
